Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy explains what happens to your details when you sign up for Return to Form, the complimentary training programme that comes with the AURASTEAK at The Rex.

Who we are

Return to Form is run by Zach Ekeke, a sole trader trading as AURASPAN, based in London, United Kingdom. AURASPAN is responsible for the personal information you give us through the signup page (the "data controller").

For any privacy question or request, email connect@auraspan.co.

What we collect

When you sign up, we collect:

  • your first name and email address;
  • which edition you chose (men's or women's);
  • whether you said yes or no to hearing from AURASPAN, and to hearing from The Rex.

Our email provider also records some technical information automatically: the IP address and approximate location you signed up from, and whether you open our emails or click the links in them.

We don't ask for health information, and you don't need to give us any to use the programme.

How we use it

To deliver your programme. We use your name, email address and edition to send you the six-week programme in stages. This is the service you asked for when you signed up, and we rely on that as our legal basis.

To keep the programme working well. We look at whether emails are delivered, opened and clicked so we can fix problems and improve the programme. We do this because it's in our legitimate interest to run the programme properly.

To send you AURASPAN news and offers, only if you ticked that box. We rely on your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time.

You don't have to agree to any marketing to get the programme.

Sharing with The Rex

We only share your details with The Rex if you ticked the box saying you'd like to hear from them. If you did, we share your name and email address so The Rex can send you its own news and offers. From that point The Rex is responsible for how it uses those details, under its own privacy policy.

If you didn't tick The Rex's box, your details are not shared with The Rex.

Who else handles your data

We use MailerLite to store your details and send our emails. MailerLite processes your information on our behalf and only under our instructions. The signup page is hosted by GitHub and loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser shares your IP address with those services when the page opens.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use legal safeguards approved under UK data protection law.

We never sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep your programme details while the programme runs and for up to 12 months after it ends, so we can answer questions and deal with any issues. After that we delete them.

If you agreed to hear from AURASPAN, we keep your name and email address for that purpose until you unsubscribe. After you unsubscribe, we keep just your email address on a suppression list so we don't contact you again by mistake.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct it, or to delete it;
  • object to us using it, or ask us to restrict how we use it;
  • ask for your information in a format you can take elsewhere;
  • withdraw your consent to marketing at any time.

To use any of these rights, email connect@auraspan.co. We'll reply within one month. You can also stop the programme emails or marketing emails at any time with the unsubscribe link at the bottom of every email.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk.

Who the programme is for

Return to Form is for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change significantly affects how we use your information, we'll tell you by email.
