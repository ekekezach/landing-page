About Me

Zach Ekeke is a London-based commercial model and personal trainer, born of Nigerian and Barbadian heritage, with campaign and editorial work for GQ, the Financial Times and New Era. He helps people elevate how they look, feel and perform.

Discipline, presence and personal standards run through everything he does — on set and in the gym. Through his coaching and guides he shares the principles that shaped his own career, because becoming your best self isn't a destination, it's a practice.

"Your journey doesn't have to start perfectly. It just has to start. And when you do start — commit to becoming someone your future self will thank you for."

— Zach Ekeke
